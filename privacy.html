Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

DueDove is operated by DJ Solutions LLC. This policy explains what we collect and why.

What we collect

  • Account data: your email address, business name and reply-to address.
  • Stripe data: for open invoices in your connected account: invoice number, amount, due date, status, payment link, and your customer's name and email.
  • Reminder records: which reminders were sent, when, and to whom, plus unsubscribes.
  • Billing: handled by Stripe. We never see your full card number.

How we use it

Only to send the reminders you configure, show your dashboard, and run your subscription. We don't sell data, use advertising trackers, or email your customers for any other purpose.

Service providers

Stripe (payments and invoice data), our email delivery provider (sending reminders), and our hosting provider in the EU (Hetzner).

Your customers

For reminders, you are the controller of your customers' data and we process it on your behalf. Your customers can unsubscribe from any reminder in one click.

Retention

When you disconnect Stripe we delete open-invoice data. Closing your account deletes your data within 30 days, except records we must keep for tax or legal reasons.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Access to your Stripe account uses Stripe's official Connect authorization, which you can revoke anytime.
