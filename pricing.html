Questions

Will my customers know it's automated?

Emails come from your business name and sound like you wrote them. Replies go straight to your inbox. You can edit every word.

What if someone already paid?

We check with Stripe or Xero right before every email. Paid, voided and canceled invoices never get a reminder.

Do you take a cut of what I collect?

No. One flat price. Your customers pay you through Stripe or Xero, the same way they do now.

Can you change anything in my account?

No. We only read invoices and customers. We never charge, refund, edit or move money in Stripe or Xero.

Do I need a card for the trial?

No. Try it free for 14 days. Add a card only if you want to keep going.

Which invoices does it remind about?

Open invoices with a due date and a customer email: Stripe invoices sent by email, or Xero sales invoices. Card-on-file subscriptions are left alone.

Do you support Xero?

Yes. Connect your Xero organisation from your DueDove dashboard. We check the balance with Xero right before every reminder.

Can I use Stripe and Xero together?

Yes. Connect either or both. Your dashboard combines them.

Do you support QuickBooks Online?

Coming soon. QuickBooks Online support is in the works but not available yet. Email support@duedove.com and we'll let you know when it's ready.
