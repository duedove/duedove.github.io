Overdue invoice reminder email templates

Six short, polite payment reminders you can copy and paste, from a friendly heads-up before the due date to a final note at 30 days. Replace the {placeholders} and send.

A reminder schedule that works

Most unpaid invoices aren't disputes. They're forgotten, stuck in someone's inbox, or waiting on the right person. A short, regular sequence fixes most of them without awkward phone calls:

  • 3 days before the due date: a heads-up with the pay link
  • On the due date
  • 3, 7 and 14 days after: friendly follow-ups that make it easy to reply
  • 30 days: a final friendly note asking to agree on next steps

The templates

1. 3 days before the due date

Subject: Invoice {number} is due on {due_date}

Hi {first_name},

A quick heads-up that invoice {number} for {amount} is due on {due_date}. You can pay it here: {pay_link}

If you've already sent payment, thank you, and please ignore this note.

Thanks,
{your_name}
{business}

2. On the due date

Subject: Invoice {number} is due today

Hi {first_name},

Just a reminder that invoice {number} for {amount} is due today. Here's the link to pay: {pay_link}

Let me know if you need a copy of the invoice or anything else from me.

Thanks,
{your_name}

3. 3 days late

Subject: Following up on invoice {number}

Hi {first_name},

I wanted to follow up on invoice {number} for {amount}, which was due on {due_date}. You can pay it here: {pay_link}

If something's holding it up, just reply and let me know. Happy to help.

Thank you,
{your_name}

4. 7 days late

Subject: Invoice {number} is now a week overdue

Hi {first_name},

Invoice {number} for {amount} was due on {due_date} and is now a week past due. Could you take a look when you get a chance? Pay link: {pay_link}

If there's a problem with the invoice, or you'd like to split the payment, reply to this email and we'll sort it out.

Thanks,
{your_name}

5. 14 days late

Subject: Second follow-up: invoice {number} ({amount})

Hi {first_name},

I haven't seen payment for invoice {number} ({amount}, due {due_date}) yet, so I'm checking in again. Here's the link: {pay_link}

If this should go to someone else on your team, could you point me to the right person?

Thank you,
{your_name}

6. 30 days late (final friendly reminder)

Subject: Invoice {number}: can we settle this?

Hi {first_name},

Invoice {number} for {amount} is now 30 days overdue. I'd like to get this resolved, and I'm glad to work with you on timing if that helps. You can pay here: {pay_link}

Please reply by {date} so we can agree on next steps.

Thank you,
{your_name}

Tips that get invoices paid

  • Always include the pay link. Every extra step (finding the invoice, logging in) delays payment.
  • Put the invoice number and amount in the subject. It's searchable and it gets forwarded to accounts payable intact.
  • Stop the moment they pay. Nothing annoys a good customer like a reminder for an invoice they paid yesterday. Check the balance right before you send.
  • Invite a reply. "If something's holding it up, let me know" turns silence into a conversation.
  • Stay factual. Don't threaten consequences you don't intend to follow through on, and follow the rules that apply to you (for example, include a way to opt out of non-essential email).
